Study S6 · Patch City Tuning · for the human trials

Can the swarm flow round the patch city's buildings and keep up with one drone?

The trials will have pilots search BlockShapedPatchCityWorld with a swarm of ten drones or with one drone. The swarm is only a fair contender if it gets through that city quickly: surrounding each building it meets, closing up again beyond it, and not breaking into pieces. S6 tunes it for that.

It defines measures of moving through the city that way, finds the spread (d_ref) that suits these buildings, and searches the obstacle-avoidance and swarm gains with the spread held constant. It confirms the pick in three cities held out of the tuning and in Unity itself. Simulation, scripted pilots flying at full stick.
