Study S9 · Street Spread · for the human trials

Does the adaptive spread narrow the swarm too much in the streets?

The trials have pilots search BlockShapedPatchCityWorld with a swarm of ten drones or with one drone. Since S8 the scene flies C35a: each building avoided as the smallest circle round its footprint, S7's slide, d_obs 0.35, and S7's adaptive spread, which narrows the formation to 0.85 of the pilot's spread in the streets and widens it back when the swarm flies at a building. In S8 it was the fastest setting in the streets, and it lost more drones than the same setting without it.

S9 is a short study (about 70 minutes) of the spread values alone, with no change to the force law: how far to narrow (0.85, 0.90, 0.95 or not at all), what the same spreads do held fixed (so the narrowed spread can be told apart from the narrowing), and the pilot's dial at its two ends. The replica on S8's harness, street-heavy routes in the scene's city and three held out, and a Unity check. Scripted pilots at full stick.

Revised twice the same day. A follow-up flew the six rows that matter four times as far, with the moment of every loss placed against what the adaptive spread was doing (and would have been doing, in the rows that hold the spread), and a second Unity bench. It overturned two of the first run's readings, and left one question open: the replica's own flights lost twice as many drones at the narrower street spread, and Unity did not. A third Unity bench, 300 fresh street routes each for 2.0 held and the scene's setting, judged by a rule written before it flew, settled that; the answer below is the settling run's.
