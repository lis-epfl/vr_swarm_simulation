S3 City Roaming

How much more does the swarm see than one drone, and where does that stop? Random walks with no task through generated Voronoi cities that vary the block size and how much of the ground is built on, flown by the single drone and by the swarm at three spreads, every condition of a walk on the same path of intent from the same start.

One of the studies in the Swarm Study Index: S1 Goal Visibility, S2 Hidden Goal Search, S3 City Roaming. Its code and output folders keep the name "clutter".

Finding a target hidden anywhere in the city

The study has no goal patches, because their geometry dominated the density study. The task-free equivalent is a target hidden at a random spot: a point at hat height anywhere on the city's open ground, found the first time a shown feed has it within 200 m with a clear sight line (the distance internal_3's pilots turned onto goals from; recognising a hat still takes 40 m, as on the other tabs). Its chance of having been found by any moment is the share of the city's open ground the walk has seen by then, so every walk gives the whole time-to-find curve. A walk lasts 5 minutes in a 730 m city, so most targets are still hidden at the end; the random-search model fitted to each walk estimates the mean time it would take.

Where a wide formation stops helping

Spreading the formation pushes its cameras apart and out toward the buildings. Two neighbouring feeds on the ring only make sense together if they share some ground at recognition range (40 m); a building between them, or a drone pressed against a facade, breaks that. These are the swarm's own measures, per spread, on the same grid: how often neighbouring feeds still share ground with the buildings in place and without them, how many feeds show only a wall, how much of the horizon no feed covers, and what the formation pays in drones and splits.
