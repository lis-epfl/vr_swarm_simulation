Study S12 · Distributed Views · for the human trials

Does the swarm find the hats faster than one drone with a 360 camera?

The swarm differs from one drone in two ways at once: its ten cameras face every way, and they stand in ten places. One drone whose 360 camera is shown on a sphere round the pilot has the first without the second, so the swarm against that drone measures what the spread-out viewpoints add beyond seeing every way at once. That is the next human comparison.

S12 flies the trials' whole task (three goals, a walking hat at each, from the start line to the third press) in the trial city with the swarm, one drone with the trials' camera and one drone with a 360 camera shown as the Antigravity A1's 2K live stream, with how far a hat and a crowd can be seen fitted to internal_4's pilots. Scripted pilots on the 2D replica, checked against Unity.
