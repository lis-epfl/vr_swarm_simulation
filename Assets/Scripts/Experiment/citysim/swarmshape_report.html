Study S8 · Footprint Avoidance · for the human trials

Should the swarm avoid each building as a tighter shape than the circle round its bounds?

The trials have pilots search BlockShapedPatchCityWorld with a swarm of ten drones or with one drone. The force law avoids each building as the circle round its axis-aligned bounding box, and in this city that circle reaches well past the facades into the streets the swarm has to fly. S7 ended by naming it the next thing to fix.

S8 adds one Inspector setting, SwarmManager.obstacleShape, with two tighter shapes: the footprint itself (Olfati-Saber's wall on each face) and the smallest circle round the footprint. Flown with S7's slide on and route stragglers off, on S7's harness: screens, a rule fixed before a four-city confirmation, square-on approaches at a wall, and the Unity bench. Scripted pilots at full stick.
