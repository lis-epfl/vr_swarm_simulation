Study S7 · Building Flow · for the human trials

Can the swarm flow round the city's buildings instead of stalling at them?

The trials have pilots search BlockShapedPatchCityWorld with a swarm of ten drones or with one drone, and the swarm earns its place by showing the pilot several sides of the streets and buildings at once while keeping up. S6 tuned its gains; three limits of how the force law treats buildings remained. Flown at a building, a drone stalls. A drone left behind one is dragged through its facade. And one spread cannot suit both the narrow streets and the buildings the swarm should surround.

S7 adds one Inspector switch on SwarmManager for each, staying with the Olfati-Saber law (no path finding): slide round buildings, route stragglers and adaptive spread. It tunes them in the replica, confirms them in the scene's city and three held out, and flies them in Unity. Scripted pilots at full stick.
