Study S10 · Ellipse Avoidance · for the human trials

Should the swarm avoid each building as an ellipse instead of the circle round it?

The trials fly BlockShapedPatchCityWorld, where the force law avoids each building as the smallest circle round its footprint (S8). A circle fits a long building badly: it lies very close to some of its walls and far from others, so how far a drone is pushed off a wall depends on which wall it is.

S10 adds an ellipse to the same setting (SwarmManager.obstacleShape Ellipse, its aspect capped by ellipseMaxAspect), fitted to each footprint so it encloses it as closely as an ellipse can, and flies it against the circle: the geometry, one drone along the walls, the formation on S8's flights, a rule fixed before a four-city confirmation, and the Unity bench. Scripted pilots at full stick.
