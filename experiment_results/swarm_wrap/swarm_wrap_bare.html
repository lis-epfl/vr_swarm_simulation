Study S11 · Wrap Hold · for the human trials

Should the swarm stay wide while it is wrapped round a building?

On the map of ERKS's first swarm trial (internal_4), the swarm widened as it surrounded the building at a goal, then narrowed while it was still round it. The adaptive spread the trial scene flies (0.85 of d_ref in the streets) keeps the full spread only while the stick's line ahead, or with the stick centred the swarm's centre itself, is near a building's middle: a swarm drifted off the middle, or a pilot steering round the building, narrows a formation that is still wrapped round it.

S11 adds a rule to the same setting (SwarmManager.spreadWrapRule): Hold keeps the full spread while any building's centre lies inside the formation, HoldUntilLeaving releases it while the pilot steers away from that building. Flown against the rule as the scene has it on ERKS's own flights, a four-city replica confirmation with a search flight calibrated on ERKS's, and the Unity bench. Scripted pilots.
