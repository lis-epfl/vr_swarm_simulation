Study S4 · Split and Surround · for the human trials

Does surrounding a block give the swarm its edge over one drone?

The human trials will set a pilot flying a ten-drone swarm against a pilot flying one drone, on a visual search through a city, and we hope to show that the swarm helps. S4 tested one way it might: arriving at a block, the swarm flows round both sides of the building and looks down every street leaving the block before the pilot chooses one. One drone passes on one side and sees only the streets on that side.

If seeing every option before choosing leads to better choices, the swarm should find a target sooner. This page follows that argument step by step, in simulation, with scripted pilots.
