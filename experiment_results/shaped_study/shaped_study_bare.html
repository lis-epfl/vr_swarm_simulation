Study S5 · Block-Shaped Surround · for the human trials

Among block-shaped buildings, does surrounding a block give the swarm its edge?

S4 asked whether flowing round a block, to look down every street leaving it before choosing, helps a pilot flying the swarm beat a pilot flying one drone on a visual search. It used small catalogue buildings. S5 asks again in BlockShapedCityWorld's city, where each building fills its block to the back of a footpath, so far less can be seen past it from a corner.

It first finds the spread (d_ref) that gets the formation round a block quickly and safely, then flies every way of taking a block at that spread on nine designs: footpath widths, block sizes, a wider street and four ways of laying out the blocks. Simulation, scripted pilots, a camera counting as seeing within 250 m.
